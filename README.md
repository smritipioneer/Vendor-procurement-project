Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing procurement transactions to provide visibility into procurement spending, vendor performance, procurement savings, and operational risks.

The objective was to create an executive-level dashboard that enables management to monitor key procurement metrics and make data-driven decisions.

The project was developed using:

Python (Pandas, NumPy)
Tableau Public
Excel
Business Problem

Procurement teams often struggle to obtain a consolidated view of procurement activities across suppliers, categories, and purchase orders.

Key business questions addressed:

How much has been spent on procurement?
How much savings have been achieved through negotiation?
Which suppliers receive the highest spend?
How many purchase orders remain open?
What procurement value is still outstanding?
Which orders require immediate attention?

Executive Procurement Dashboard
Purpose

Provide management with a high-level overview of procurement performance and spending trends.

KPI Cards
Total Spend

Total procurement expenditure across all purchase orders.

Total Savings

Savings achieved through negotiated pricing compared to standard prices.

Open PO Count

Number of purchase orders that remain open.

Open PO Value

Total value of outstanding purchase orders.

High Risk Orders

Orders identified as requiring management attention based on predefined risk criteria.

Dashboard Visualizations
Monthly Spend Trend

Tracks procurement spending over time to identify spending patterns and seasonal trends.

Top Vendors by Spend

Identifies suppliers receiving the highest procurement spend.

Spend by Category

Highlights procurement expenditure across different item categories.

Procurement Summary

Provides an executive view of procurement activity and key business metrics.

Key Insights Generated
Identified top suppliers contributing the majority of procurement spend.
Measured procurement savings achieved through negotiated pricing.
Highlighted outstanding procurement commitments through open purchase orders.
Provided visibility into procurement spending trends.
Flagged procurement transactions requiring further review.
Business Impact

The dashboard enables procurement managers and business leaders to:

Monitor procurement performance.
Improve spend visibility.
Track procurement savings.
Manage open purchase order exposure.
Identify procurement risks proactively.
